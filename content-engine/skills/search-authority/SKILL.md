# Search Authority

Build a compounding market-intelligence and product-discovery system, not a traffic farm.

This skill sits **upstream** of `content-creation`. A writer is activated only after a page passes the commission gate. Search demand can propose a page; it cannot justify one by itself.

## Required Inputs

Capture these before planning:

- **Brand and canonical domain**
- **Category thesis** — the durable territory the brand intends to own
- **Commercial outcome** — product, assessment, tool, subscription, affiliate path, or trust outcome
- **Primary audience and job-to-be-done**
- **Existing URLs, products, research, tools, media, and first-party evidence**
- **Known demand signals** — analytics, Search Console, customer language, sales questions, community signals, or SEO-provider data
- **Constraints** — publication capacity, evidence availability, review requirements, and refresh budget

When several brands are involved, create the ownership map before keyword research.

## Authority Contract

Every cluster must obey these rules:

1. **One intent, one canonical owner, one canonical URL.**
2. A sibling brand may publish an application page only when its reader job, evidence, offer, and angle are materially different.
3. **No page-per-keyword-variant publishing.** Closely related formulations belong in one substantial page.
4. **Artifact before prose.** Each commissioned page needs original evidence, a framework, decision tool, benchmark, template, diagram, code, or documented experiment.
5. The offer and primary action are designed in the brief, not attached after writing.
6. The initial proof wave is capped at three publishable assets.
7. Every page has a named owner, refresh trigger, measurement window, and merge-or-kill path.
8. A page is not complete at publication. It is complete after evidence from the learning window changes the next decision.

## Signal Hierarchy

Use the strongest available evidence in this order:

1. First-party query, analytics, site-search, product-use, CRM, and conversion data
2. Direct customer language, support questions, sales calls, interviews, and community conversations
3. Existing product capabilities, internal research, benchmarks, and operating evidence
4. Search-result structure, competitor coverage, and trusted SEO-provider data
5. Social or trend signals
6. Intuition

For each important signal, record the **source, date, confidence, and decision it affects**. Never turn estimated keyword volume into asserted fact.

## Workflow

### 1. Inventory the Existing Estate

Audit before ideation:

- current URLs and their apparent intent
- rankings, impressions, clicks, conversions, and assisted conversions when available
- overlapping titles, sections, and internal links
- existing products, assessments, tools, source material, visuals, code, and case evidence
- pages that should be refreshed, consolidated, redirected, or retired

Output an inventory with: `keep`, `refresh`, `merge`, `redirect`, `retire`, or `unresolved`.

### 2. Define Category Ownership

Create a portfolio map:

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Category | Durable semantic territory |
| Canonical brand | Brand with authority and commercial right to own it |
| Canonical hub | Existing or proposed URL |
| Audience job | Decision or outcome the page must enable |
| Product bridge | Where qualified demand should go next |
| Sibling-brand rule | Distinct application angles allowed elsewhere |
| Collision risks | Existing URLs or brands that may cannibalize it |

Do not proceed while ownership is ambiguous.

### 3. Build the Intent–Entity–Job Graph

Map queries as a graph rather than a flat list.

For each node capture:

- entity or concept
- search intent: learn, compare, decide, implement, troubleshoot, evaluate, or buy
- audience and job-to-be-done
- stage of awareness
- canonical URL or proposed page
- parent and sibling relationships
- evidence or artifact required
- offer bridge
- freshness requirement

Use the query grammar to discover hypotheses:

```text
[capability]
+ for [specific role]
+ using [tool or architecture]
+ to achieve [measurable job]
+ under [constraint]
+ in [time context]
```

The grammar proposes research. It does not authorize pages.

### 4. Run the Page Commission Gate

Score every proposed page from 0–2 on eight dimensions:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business relevance | Decorative | Indirect | Direct strategic value |
| Demand evidence | Assumed | Weak signal | Multiple credible signals |
| Intent distinctness | Duplicative | Partly distinct | Clear standalone reader job |
| Differentiated thesis | Commodity | Improved synthesis | Defensible point of view |
| Evidence | None | Third-party only | First-party or inspectable evidence |
| Executable artifact | None | Checklist only | Tool, framework, benchmark, code, template, or experiment |
| Offer bridge | Unclear | Generic CTA | Natural next action with value continuity |
| Maintenance fitness | No owner | Manual hope | Owner, trigger, and review window defined |

**Commission threshold: 12/16.** A zero in intent distinctness, evidence, or executable artifact is an automatic hold.

Rejected ideas must be merged into an existing page, kept as a section or FAQ, turned into social content, held for more evidence, or killed.

### 5. Select a Three-Asset Proof Wave

Prefer this portfolio:

1. **Category authority page** — defines the model and owns the broad intent
2. **Decision or implementation asset** — comparison, calculator, assessment, configurator, tutorial, or architecture guide
3. **Proof asset** — benchmark, documented build, case study, experiment, or operating log

Choose the smallest wave capable of validating category demand and offer resonance. Do not plan the twenty-page cluster first.

### 6. Produce the Page Commission Packet

Each commissioned page must include:

- canonical owner, URL hypothesis, parent hub, and internal-link role
- audience, job, intent, awareness stage, and exclusion boundary
- primary query set and entity coverage
- thesis, counter-position, and why this page should exist
- evidence ledger with source, date, confidence, and claim mapping
- original artifact contract
- answer units: concise sections that resolve specific reader decisions
- visual and direct-media requirements
- primary and secondary action
- distribution derivatives and channel-specific angle
- technical requirements: title, description, canonical, schema, sitemap, robots, mobile, performance, analytics events
- author, reviewer, maintainer, refresh trigger, and measurement dates
- commission score and decision: `PASS`, `HOLD`, `MERGE`, or `KILL`

### 7. Assemble the Editorial Cell

Do not ask one model to be researcher, writer, skeptic, SEO editor, designer, and marketer simultaneously.

Use explicit roles:

- **Commissioning editor** — owns business relevance and page existence
- **Demand intelligence analyst** — owns query and market evidence
- **Research lead** — owns sources, claims, provenance, and direct evidence
- **Author** — owns argument and reader experience
- **Skeptic editor** — attacks unsupported claims and false novelty
- **Search architect** — owns canonical graph, answer structure, schema, and internal links
- **Commercial editor** — owns offer continuity and conversion logic
- **Visual director** — owns diagrams, screenshots, original media, and accessibility
- **Production engineer** — owns repository implementation, preview, QA, and release evidence
- **Performance analyst** — owns learning windows and expand/refresh/merge/kill recommendations

A small team may combine roles, but the reviews remain separate passes.

### 8. Hand Off to Production

Only pages marked `PASS` move into `content-creation`.

Production order:

1. Research and evidence packet
2. Original artifact or direct media
3. Outline and answer-unit architecture
4. Draft in the active brand voice
5. Independent claim, search, commercial, visual, and human-quality reviews
6. Repository branch and pull request
7. Rendered preview and mobile/technical QA
8. Publication and production verification
9. Distribution from channel-native derivatives
10. Measurement and learning review

No direct publish authority is implied by this skill.

### 9. Measure at Three Windows

**T+7 — discoverability and release quality**

- indexation and crawl behavior
- impressions and query breadth
- technical or rendering defects
- distribution reach and early engagement

**T+28 — intent and conversion quality**

- qualified clicks and engaged reading
- tool, assessment, template, or email completions
- internal-link progression
- primary and assisted conversion
- emerging queries and missing answer units

**T+90 — authority and economics**

- durable ranking and citation behavior
- branded and non-branded demand movement
- conversion and assisted revenue
- refresh burden
- whether the cluster should expand, consolidate, reposition, or stop

Never reward content volume. Reward qualified discovery, capability delivered, conversion continuity, and reusable evidence.

## Required Outputs

Return these artifacts in order:

1. **Category decision memo**
2. **Canonical ownership and cannibalization map**
3. **Intent–entity–job–URL graph**
4. **Commission scorecard for every proposed page**
5. **Three-asset proof wave**
6. **One complete page commission packet per approved asset**
7. **Production and review assignments**
8. **Distribution design**
9. **T+7, T+28, and T+90 measurement contract**
10. **Expansion, refresh, merge, and kill rules**

## Failure Conditions

Stop or hold when:

- the canonical owner is unresolved
- an existing page already satisfies the same job
- the only differentiation is wording or length
- no inspectable evidence or executable artifact exists
- the CTA changes subject instead of continuing the reader's job
- the page depends on unverifiable claims or fabricated experience
- the cluster cannot be maintained
- publication is being used to avoid building the actual product, tool, benchmark, or proof

The best search page is often a product surface with an argument around it. The best keyword is the language the market uses for a problem the system genuinely solves.
