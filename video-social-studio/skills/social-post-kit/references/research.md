# How existing tools package posts, and where this skill differs

Read 2026-09-29. The bar: a creator should get a better post package from this skill than from the
caption helper inside her scheduler, and should never be told something the tool can't know.

## Sources

1. **Buffer AI Assistant** - https://buffer.com/resources/introducing-buffers-ai-assistant/
   Generates and repurposes posts, adapts tone per channel ("LinkedIn posts into Twitter threads"),
   and lets users "set guidelines for the tone and style of posts" for a consistent voice. Free on
   every plan. It works from text you paste; the announcement says nothing about starting from a
   video transcript, and it does not produce alt text, on-screen titles or a pre-post checklist.

2. **Later, best time to post** - https://later.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-instagram/
   6M+ posts analysed. Headline times (Reels at 12 AM Monday) with Later's own caveat: "Take these
   times with a grain of salt ... finding your unique posting time matters most." Its in-app
   feature calculates times from your best posts of the last month. Metricool's 2026 study of 2M+
   posts gives different windows (Instagram 6-9 AM, TikTok 6-8 PM):
   https://metricool.com/best-time-to-post-social-networks/

3. **Opus Clip** - https://opus-clip.com/ and the independent test at
   https://bigvu.tv/blog/opus-clip-tested-2026-where-ai-wins-40-percent-discard/
   Cuts a long video into 10+ clips, reframes to 9:16, burns captions, gives each clip a 0-99
   "virality score", and schedules to TikTok, Reels and Shorts. The independent test reports
   discarding roughly 40% of the clips. The score is a model's guess presented as a number.

## What this skill does better

| Gap in the tools above | What social-post-kit does |
|---|---|
| Captions start from a pasted blurb, so they describe the video from outside | Starts from her own words in the SRT or transcript. The hook line is a sentence she actually said, so the caption sounds like her |
| Voice is a tone slider or a guideline box | Saves her niche, audience, voice words, phrases she uses and phrases she hates to `creator-profile.md` once, and reuses content-engine's voice file when it exists |
| Numbers presented as predictions (virality score, "best time") | No scores. Timing is written as a hypothesis with a test plan, and the Friday review in content-calendar checks it against her own results |
| Alt text and on-screen title are left to the creator | Every package includes alt text for the cover/first frame and an on-screen title that fits the safe box from platform-specs |
| One caption stretched across platforms | Six packages, each within that platform's sourced character and hashtag limits, with the part shown before "more" doing the work |
| No pre-flight check | A per-platform checklist (length, aspect, file type, text in safe zone, music rights, hashtags counted) so nothing gets rejected after upload |

What the tools do that this skill does not: publish, schedule, and read analytics automatically.
The content-calendar skill exports a CSV she can import into Buffer, Later or Metricool.
