# Platform specs, September 2026

Read date for every row: **2026-09-29**. Platforms change these numbers without notice, so re-check
any row older than 90 days before relying on it. Tags:

- **[OFFICIAL]** read on the platform's own help page or its official account.
- **[TRADE]** consistent across several third-party guides, no official page reached.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** one source only, sources disagree, or the number moves with app version.

Default export for every vertical platform: **1080 x 1920, 9:16, H.264 + AAC, MP4, 30 fps.**
That one file covers Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Facebook Reels. LinkedIn and X also accept it.

## 3. Hashtags

| Platform | Rule | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | **Hard cap of 5 hashtags per post and Reel** (since December 2025). Instagram: "Using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones, can improve both your content's performance and people's experience." | [OFFICIAL, quoted from @creators via Social Media Today 2025-12-18] | https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/ |
| YouTube | More than **60** hashtags and YouTube ignores all of them. Up to 3 of the description hashtags show by the title. Repetitive tags can get a video removed. Several blogs say "15": the official page says 60. | [OFFICIAL] | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658 |
| TikTok | No published cap inside the 4,000-character caption. 3-5 specific tags is common guidance. | [UNVERIFIED] | https://typecount.com/blog/tiktok-caption-character-limit |
| LinkedIn | No cap. 1-3 relevant tags is common guidance. | [UNVERIFIED] | https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-character-limit |
| X | No cap; tags eat the 280 characters. 0-2 is enough. | [UNVERIFIED] | https://typecount.com/blog/twitter-character-limit |
| Facebook | No cap; 0-3 is enough. | [UNVERIFIED] | https://postfa.st/sizes/facebook/reels |

The plugin's rule: **3 to 5 hashtags, specific over broad, never more than Instagram's 5 on any platform.**
One cap is easier for a creator to remember than six.

## 4. Safe zones for on-screen text (1080 x 1920 frame)

No platform publishes one fixed pixel table for organic posts, and TikTok's own ad docs say the
safe area shrinks as the caption gets longer. All numbers below are **[UNVERIFIED]** working margins
taken from the most conservative of the sources.

| Platform | Keep clear: top | Keep clear: bottom | Keep clear: sides | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram / Facebook Reels | 14% (~270 px) | 35% (~670 px) | 6% each (~65 px) | https://billo.app/blog/meta-ads-safe-zones/ (Meta's unified Stories/Reels zone, March 2026, stated on a 1440x2560 canvas and scaled here) |
| TikTok | ~130-200 px | ~480 px | right ~180 px (like/comment/share rail), left ~60 px | https://zeely.ai/blog/tiktok-safe-zones/ · https://checksafe.zone/articles/tiktok-safe-area-overlay-guide-2026 |
| YouTube Shorts | ~180 px | ~350 px | right ~120 px | https://postplanify.com/tools/youtube-shorts-safe-zone-checker |

**One safe box that works on all three:** keep titles and burned captions inside
**x 65-900 px, y 270-1250 px**. That is roughly the upper-middle of the frame, clear of the bottom
third where every app puts its caption bar, handle and music label. `vid.mjs burn --position middle`
targets this box.
